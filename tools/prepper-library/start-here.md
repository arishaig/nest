## Tags in book titles

- **[old medicine]**: historical medical advice. Doses and remedies may be wrong
  or dangerous. Check against *Where There Is No Doctor*.
- **[old canning]**: written before modern food-safety rules. Use the USDA
  *Complete Guide to Home Canning* for times and methods.
- **[verify ID]**: wild plant or mushroom identification. Never eat anything on
  the word of a single book; lookalikes kill.

Some medical books also come with an **(original PDF)** copy next to them. Use it
when a table or dosage chart looks broken in the book version.

## Battery

Turn on airplane mode / turn Wi-Fi off to make the battery last for weeks. It
charges from any USB charger or power bank.
